What the yellow cards mean
Sentence Stems
Structured starts that help students finish a thought in correct academic form.
Speaking Stems
Starters for talk: "I notice…", "I agree because…"
Prompt Stem
A trigger for a student to extend or self-repair thinking: "Tell me more about…"
Must-Use Vocab
A short word bank students are required to use in a response.
Highlight
Mark the structure: commas, units, key words.
Talk Read Talk Write
Talk, read a chunk, talk again, then write. Chunk size fits the learners.
Chunking
Break a text or task into smaller pieces.
Organizer
A chart or template that holds and orders thinking, such as a place value chart.
